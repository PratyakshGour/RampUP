# New employee onboarding — 2026-10-01

Priya Nair, the Payments team lead, welcomed Riya Sharma, a new backend engineer, to the Atlas Payments project. Riya, Priya, and Arjun Mehta attended the meeting.

Priya assigned Riya to review the payment event flow before her first sprint. Arjun will pair with Riya on the Kafka consumer runbook. The team uses Kafka for payment events and PostgreSQL for transactional payment records.

An earlier proposal suggested validating access tokens inside the Payments API. The team has not approved that proposal; the authentication handoff will resolve the boundary.
